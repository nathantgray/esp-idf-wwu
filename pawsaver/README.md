## Safety Thresholds

| Temperature (°F) | Temperature (°C) | Status  | Recommendation         |
|------------------|------------------|---------|------------------------|
| Below 100°F      | Below 38°C       | ✅ Safe  | Safe to walk           |
| 100°F – 120°F    | 38°C – 49°C      | ⚠️ Caution | Limit walk duration |
| Above 120°F      | Above 49°C       | 🚫 Danger | Do not walk            |
